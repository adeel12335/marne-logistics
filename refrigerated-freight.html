Shipment planning

A clear plan before the trailer is booked.

Temperature-sensitive freight needs more than an origin and a destination. Product handling instructions, loading conditions, shipment timing and equipment suitability all belong in the initial conversation. A complete brief gives everyone the same reference point before a commitment is made.

Marne Logistics LLC is an Atlanta-based motor-carrier business with a focus that includes refrigerated freight opportunities. Specific equipment, operating range, monitoring arrangements and lane availability must be confirmed for each load. This page does not promise a particular temperature range or monitoring service.

Your questions, answered

Temperature-Controlled Freight FAQs

Which temperature range can be supported?

The supported range depends on the equipment and shipment requirements. Provide the required set point and handling instructions so suitability can be confirmed before booking.

Does a quote include temperature monitoring?

Monitoring, record availability and communication expectations must be agreed for the specific shipment. Do not assume a particular reporting service is included.

What should I send for a refrigerated freight inquiry?

Include the commodity, route, requested dates, shipment size and shipper-provided temperature instructions. The refrigerated shipping guide provides a more detailed checklist.
