Shipment planning

Coverage follows the shipment requirements.

A useful lane review considers more than mileage. Appointment times, equipment needs, facility access, loading readiness and delivery requirements all influence whether a transport plan is workable. Sharing the full picture helps avoid assumptions about the route.

Marne Logistics is based in Atlanta, Georgia. Regional and long-haul service requests are evaluated individually and depend on equipment, capacity, operating authority and scheduling. No fixed nationwide coverage, guaranteed route or standing capacity is represented here.

Your questions, answered

Regional & Long-Haul Freight FAQs

Which states or lanes are served?

Specific service coverage must be confirmed for the shipment. Send the origin, destination and dates for review rather than assuming a lane is available.

Can I request a time-sensitive shipment?

You can include time-sensitive requirements in an inquiry. The schedule must be reviewed before any transit or delivery commitment is made.

Should I include a return lane?

If you are discussing recurring movements, include relevant return routes or shipment patterns. They can be considered as part of the lane discussion.
