A plain-language notice covering inquiry preparation, local downloads and the features currently included on this website.

This notice describes the current inquiry and content features of this website. It applies to the browser-based inquiry preparation tool provided here.

Information entered in the inquiry form

The form asks for contact and inquiry information so you can create a text file on your own device. The current form does not transmit the entered values to Marne Logistics, send email or store submissions in an online application database.

The generated file may contain the details you entered. You control where it is saved and whether you share it. Please do not enter sensitive identity, banking, tax or other confidential records.

Cookies and analytics

The website source does not include advertising tags, third-party analytics or application cookies. The hosting platform may process technical requests and use its own access or authentication features under its applicable policies.

Downloads and resources

Planning worksheets are downloadable files. They do not collect responses or send completed copies back to the company. Images are hosted with the website rather than requested from external stock-photo services.

Future changes to inquiry delivery

If an email service, customer relationship system, analytics service or online submission storage is connected, this notice will need to reflect the actual collection, use, retention and contact arrangements before that feature is used.
